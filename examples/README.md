## Publication figure gallery

These are synthetic drawing and layout examples, not measured results,
experimental procedures, or proposed reaction mechanisms. The three PNGs are
600 DPI exports. Preserve their aspect ratio and review text at the intended
insertion size against your journal's requirements; resolution alone does not
establish publication suitability.

### Molecule pair

Two anisole drawings with aligned captions, subscript identifiers and a
superscript footnote mark. [Download PNG](publication-pair.png).

![Two anisole drawings with aligned captions](publication-pair.png)

### Independent molecular parts

A caption shared by two independently aligned molecular parts, alongside a
single-molecule block. [Download PNG](publication-independent-parts.png).

![Independently aligned molecular parts with a shared caption](publication-independent-parts.png)

### Two-row comparison

Two complete symbolic input–output rows with common column widths and labelled
arrows. [Download PNG](publication-comparison.png).

![Two symbolic input-output rows with aligned columns](publication-comparison.png)

To edit or resize these drawings, follow the [publication figure recipes](../docs/PUBLICATION_SCHEMES.md).
They create `.chemvas` originals, SVG, PNG and layout/font reports using public
Chemvas commands, without optional RDKit. The recipes preserve a common physical
scale rather than stretching every figure to one width.

## Start drawing

Open [first-scheme.chemvas](first-scheme.chemvas) via **File ▸ Open** for a small
benzyl alcohol oxidation drawing you can edit immediately.
[Walkthrough](../docs/FIRST_SCHEME.md) · [한국어](../docs/FIRST_SCHEME.ko.md) ·
[SVG](first-scheme.svg) · [PNG](first-scheme.png).

This is also a schematic exercise, not an experimental procedure. Its SVG and
300 DPI PNG use the 174 mm column setting.
The editable drawing opens and exports with the core installation; recreating
its SMILES insertion steps requires optional RDKit.

These examples are separate downloads and are not included in the Python package.
